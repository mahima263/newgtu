Student Scholarship & Academic Opportunity

Expanding Access, Exposure and Excellence

IIT Kanpur’s student community is defined by talent, ambition and the ability to contribute far beyond the classroom. Yet meaningful academic growth often depends on opportunities that are not equally accessible to every student.

The Student Scholarship & Academic Opportunity opportunity brings together scholarships, travel grants, student exchange programmes and awards within one integrated support framework. Its purpose is to ensure that financial circumstances do not limit a student’s ability to study, participate, compete, collaborate or represent IIT Kanpur on national and global platforms.

Through philanthropic support, students can gain access to experiences that deepen learning, broaden perspective and strengthen their confidence to pursue ambitious academic and professional paths.

Enabling Talent to Go Further

A scholarship can allow a deserving student to focus more fully on education. A travel grant can help a researcher present work at an international conference. A student exchange can provide exposure to new academic environments, cultures and ways of thinking. An award can recognise excellence and encourage a student to continue pursuing difficult goals.

These interventions may appear different, but they serve a common purpose: helping students make the most of their potential.

For many students, the difference between being selected for an opportunity and being able to accept it comes down to financial support. Student Scholarship & Academic Opportunity seeks to close that gap by making high-value academic and developmental experiences more accessible.

About Student Scholarship & Academic Opportunity

The opportunity supports students across
four priority areas:

01. Scholarships

Need-based and merit-based support that enables students to continue their education with dignity and focus.

02. Travel Grants

Assistance for students presenting research, participating in competitions, attending conferences or representing IIT Kanpur at significant academic and professional forums.

03. Student Exchange Programmes

Support for academic exchanges, international exposure and collaborative learning with leading institutions in India and abroad.

04. Awards and Recognition

Recognition for academic excellence, research, leadership, innovation, service and exceptional student achievement.

Together, these initiatives can strengthen academic performance, research exposure, leadership development and global engagement across the student community.

Philanthropic support can help enable:

  • Merit- and need-based scholarships

  • Domestic and international travel grants

  • Conference and competition participation

  • Student exchange and mobility programmes

  • Research and innovation awards

  • Leadership and excellence recognition

  • Support for students facing financial barriers
