The IIT Kanpur Campaign

IIT Kanpur has always been about something larger than the sum of its people. Bigger than any single idea, discovery, or name on a building. Greater Than Us is the campaign that matches that vision: bold investments in research, infrastructure, and talent that will define what India becomes.

The institution is writing its most ambitious chapter yet and it is asking you to be a part of it.

I was no longer myself but an instrument of a historical process, as if possessed by something greater than me.

When P.K. Kelkar founded this institution in 1959, he gave it a disposition that has been a guiding force: the belief that education here must serve something larger than any individual ambition. Greater Than Us is that belief, made urgent again; for an India at its most pivotal moment.

Three Bets on India

Through IIT Kanpur

For sixty years, IIT Kanpur has been at the frontier of what India can become; building the knowledge, the technology, the healthcare systems, and the leaders that a self-reliant nation requires.

The Greater Than Us campaign concentrates that ambition into three priorities; each one a domain where IITK's contribution is not just valuable, but irreplaceable. This is a prelude to IIT Kanpur’s next chapter.
