Investing in India’s Cyber Resilience

C3iHub

C3iHub translates IIT Kanpur’s cybersecurity research into deployable technologies, critical-infrastructure solutions, startups and specialised workforce programmes.

As India’s infrastructure, industries and public services become increasingly connected, cybersecurity is no longer only about protecting information. It is also about safeguarding the physical systems that power factories, move goods, deliver essential services and support national security.

Established at IIT Kanpur with support from the Department of Science and Technology, Government of India, C3iHub is translating cybersecurity research into deployable technologies, industry solutions, startups and skilled talent.

When Cyberattacks Affect the Physical World

Power systems, manufacturing plants, ports, telecom networks and refineries increasingly depend on connected software, sensors and operational controls.

These connections improve efficiency, but they also create new vulnerabilities. A cyberattack can disrupt machinery, alter operating conditions, compromise safety or interrupt an essential service.

Protecting such infrastructure requires more than conventional cybersecurity tools. Defenders must understand how software, networks and physical processes interact—and identify abnormal behaviour before a digital intrusion produces real-world consequences.

C3iHub was created around this challenge.

A National Investment in Cybersecurity Capability

C3iHub was established under the Government of India’s National Mission on Interdisciplinary Cyber-Physical Systems.

Structured as a not-for-profit Section 8 company, it brings research, technology development, industrial deployment, startup support and workforce training together within one platform.

This public investment enabled IIT Kanpur to address cybersecurity as a national capability rather than through isolated projects.

From Research to Operational Deployment

Technologies that work in controlled research environments require significant refinement before they can protect complex industrial systems.

C3iHub works with organisations responsible for critical infrastructure, helping researchers understand operational constraints and adapt technologies for real deployment.

Its work includes intrusion detection, malware analysis, deception-based security, industrial-control-system protection and integrated monitoring of information-technology and operational-technology environments.

One major example is the establishment of integrated security-monitoring capability at Bhilai Steel Plant, strengthening visibility across digital and plant systems.

Building Technologies, Enterprises and Talent

India’s cybersecurity requirements cannot depend entirely on imported products.

C3iHub supports indigenous technologies and helps research teams develop them into deployable products. Startups receive access to technology development, mentoring, industry engagement and commercialisation pathways.

The Hub also develops cybersecurity maturity frameworks that help organisations assess preparedness, identify weaknesses and build long-term resilience.

Its skilling programmes train students, professionals and government personnel in areas including network security, malware analysis, cryptography, digital forensics and cyber defence.

Securing India’s Connected Future

C3iHub has evolved into a Technology Translational Research Park, strengthening its role as a bridge between academic research and large-scale application.

Its value lies in how each part reinforces the others: research creates tools, translation makes them usable, startups help them scale, training enables deployment and operational experience generates new research questions.

As India builds a more connected future, C3iHub is helping ensure that security is built into its foundations.
